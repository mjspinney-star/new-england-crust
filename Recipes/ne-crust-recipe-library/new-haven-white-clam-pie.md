The white clam pie is the single most famous pizza in New England, the one that makes national best-of lists every year and inspires two-hour lines in New Haven. And here's the secret: it's one of the easier pizzas in this whole library to make. No sauce to balance, no cheese pull to chase — just fresh clams, garlic, and a brutally hot oven.

Living this close to the clam flats of the North Shore, we have no excuse not to use fresh littlenecks at least once.
