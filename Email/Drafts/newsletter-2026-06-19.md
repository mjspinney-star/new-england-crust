We pulled a margherita off the stone this weekend and held the olive oil until it was sitting on the cutting board, not the peel. One drizzle, and the peppery bite actually came through instead of vanishing into the scorched crust. Small change, completely different pizza.

Here's the thing: finishing oil only earns its place on certain pies — margherita, white pies, anything with burrata or fresh mozzarella dropped on after the bake. If you've got sausage, pepperoni, and a heavy cheese pull going on, skip it — there's already enough fat in play and the oil just disappears. Timing matters as much as the pie itself: pour it the moment the pizza comes off the stone, not while it's still waiting on the peel. And check your bottle while you're at it — if it's been open for eight months by the stove, that's not finishing oil anymore, it's just rancid.
