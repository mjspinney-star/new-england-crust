There's a moment on a good night when the Ninja Woodfire has been preheating for twenty minutes, the infrared thermometer reads about 540°F on the stone, and the first pizza goes in. Five minutes later it comes out golden, and the whole night settles in.

**New England Crust** is a place to write that down.

## What we'll cover

Three threads:

- **Recipes.** [Cold-fermented dough](/blog/72-hour-cold-ferment-dough/) made with Caputo 00, the [pizzas we rotate through](/blog/pizza-night-recipes-beyond-margherita/), and the regional styles New England actually has an argument about: [New Haven tomato pie](/recipes/new-haven-plain-tomato-pie/), [South Shore bar pizza](/recipes/south-shore-bar-pizza/), [Greek pizza](/recipes/greek-pizza/), [beach pizza](/recipes/beach-pizza/), and the [clam pie](/recipes/clam-pie-new-england-way/).
- **The oven.** We cook on a Ninja Woodfire OO101: electric, with pellets for smoke. It makes our pizza, and it also smokes pork shoulder and chicken, which matters when the menu wanders past pizza. [Here's why we chose it](/blog/best-outdoor-pizza-ovens-under-500/).
- **Gear, honestly.** What we [actually use](/gear/), what's [worth buying](/blog/ninja-woodfire-accessories-worth-buying/), and what's a waste. When we mention gear we haven't used ourselves, we say so.

> The dough is the project. The oven is the season.

## What we won't do

We won't pretend we've cooked on ovens we haven't, and we won't pretend a backyard oven is a Naples sidewalk. We're cooking on a deck in New England, and that's the whole point.

Glad you're here. Pull up a chair.
