## How pizza oven deals actually work

Retailers discount across categories during the big events, but the deals aren't uniform. Big-ticket outdoor cooking gear tends to see meaningful discounts — 15–25% on ovens in the $300–$500 range isn't unusual. Accessories in the $20–$50 range often drop too, sometimes more aggressively than the hardware.

What doesn't move much: niche items with small inventory, and anything that's already been discounted recently. Pricing algorithms are aware of their own history.

You don't need a special "deals page" at the retailer, either. If you're watching a product and the price drops during an event, that's the deal. The links below go straight to the product pages — check them during any event and you'll see whatever's running.

## What we'd skip

Branded "pizza night" bundles — the ones with a branded peel, a cutter, and a serving board with the oven's logo on it. They're marked up to sell. You can buy better individual versions of each item for less.

Anything sold as a "pizza oven cover set" with multiple sizes. Find the cover made for your oven model. Generic covers that "fit most ovens" usually don't fit any oven well.

## The sale calendar

The windows worth watching, in order through the year:

### Prime Big Deal Days (October 6–7, 2026)
Amazon's fall event — 48 hours, Prime members only. It's smaller than the summer Prime Day, but outdoor cooking gear often gets end-of-season pricing on top of event pricing. If you can cook through a New England fall (you can — it's half the fun), this is the sleeper window.

### Black Friday & Cyber Monday (late November)
The deepest oven discounts of the year, and the window when the brands themselves — Ooni and Solo Stove especially — run their biggest sales on their own sites alongside the retailers. If you're gifting an oven for the holidays, buy it here, not in December.

### Prime Day (summer)
Amazon's big summer event — it ran June 23–26 in 2026, earlier than its usual July slot. Historically one of the best windows for the Ninja Woodfire and for accessories.

### Everything else
One-off flash sales happen year-round, especially on accessories. That's what the price signals above are for: if a listed price beats the signal, it's a deal regardless of what the calendar says.
