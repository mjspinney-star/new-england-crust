Prime Day runs June 23–26 this year. Four days. If you've been sitting on a decision about an outdoor pizza oven — or you've been eyeing an accessory you keep talking yourself out of — this is the window worth paying attention to.

A few things to know before we get into specifics.

## What actually goes on sale

Amazon discounts across categories during Prime Day, but the deals aren't uniform. Big-ticket kitchen and outdoor cooking gear tends to see meaningful discounts — 15–25% on ovens in the $300–$500 range isn't unusual. Accessories in the $20–$50 range often drop too, sometimes more aggressively than the hardware.

What doesn't move much: highly niche items with small inventory, and anything that's already been discounted recently. Amazon's pricing algorithms are aware of their own history.

The other thing worth knowing: you don't need to find a specific "Prime Day deal" page. If you're watching a product and the price drops during June 23–26, that's the deal. The affiliate links below point directly to the product pages — check them during the event and you'll see whatever Amazon is running.

## The accessories that are actually worth buying on sale

Pizza oven accessories are where Prime Day can quietly save you more money than the hardware. A few worth having on your radar:

**[Metal turning peel](https://amzn.to/3SeASJE)** — Not optional. You need one to rotate your pizza inside the oven. The good ones run $30–$40. If you see a well-reviewed perforated aluminum peel drop below $25, that's an easy purchase.

**[Digital kitchen scale](https://amzn.to/4uHkrmS)** — Dough by weight is more consistent than dough by volume. Every time. A decent scale runs $10–$20 normally, often less on Prime Day.

**[Dough proofing container](https://amzn.to/4vwK9vD)** — Not strictly necessary, but genuinely more convenient than a bowl and plastic wrap. Measurement markings, a lid that actually seals, stackable. The kind of thing that costs $15–$20 and you wonder why you waited.

**Infrared thermometer** — Already mentioned in our oven comparison, but worth repeating here: this $20 tool tells you your actual stone temperature, not the air temperature your oven's display shows. The gap between those two numbers is where bad pizza comes from. Buy one now if you don't have one.

## What we'd skip

Branded "pizza night" bundles — the ones that come with a branded peel, a cutter, and a serving board with your oven's logo on it. They're marked up to sell. You can buy better individual versions of each item for less.

Anything marketed as a "pizza oven cover set" with multiple sizes — find the specific cover made for your oven model. Generic covers that "fit most ovens" usually don't fit any oven well.

## When to check

Prime Day starts June 23. The best deals on specific items tend to appear in the first 12–18 hours and then again in the final stretch on the 26th. The middle period sometimes sees restocks and lightning deals on accessories.
