Fresh basil or dried on your pizza? It depends entirely on how your oven cooks. At 900–950°F in an Ooni Koda 12, basil on the pie before the bake turns black and bitter in under a minute — not charred, just scorched. Our fix: pull the pie, let it rest 30 seconds, then tear the leaves on top. Residual heat wilts them without the burn. Dried basil has its place too — stirred into sauce and given time to bloom, it adds a low background note on longer, lower-heat cooks. It just doesn't belong on top of a 950°F pie.

Rule we go by: fresh after the bake for high-heat outdoor pizza, dried in the sauce for everything else.
