00 or bread flour — it's the debate we hear most in our own backyard. Short answer: neither one is "better," they just do different jobs. 00 flour is milled finer with less protein, about 11 to 12 percent, so it stretches easy by hand and bakes up light — that's why it earns its keep in a hot, fast oven like an Ooni Koda 12 running near 900°F. Bread flour runs higher, 12.5 to 14 percent protein, which means more chew and more structure — the better call if you're cooking cooler, like a Solo Stove Pi or anything in the 700 to 800°F range, or piling on heavy toppings. We sometimes blend 70 percent 00 with 30 percent bread flour for a middle ground. No trick to it — just match the flour to how hot your oven actually runs.

Rule we go by: high heat and a fast cook, use 00. Lower and slower, bread flour won't let you down.
