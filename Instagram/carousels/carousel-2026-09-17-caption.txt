We bought too many Ninja Woodfire accessories in the first month. Half of them are still sitting in a kitchen drawer.

Here's what we'd actually buy again: a cover, a 12-gauge outdoor extension cord (the stock cord is only about 4.5 feet), a real perforated peel, and an infrared thermometer. All four together run under $80 — and that thermometer matters more than it sounds like it should, since the oven's display reads air temperature, not stone temperature, and those two numbers can differ by 100°F or more.

Everything else — the stand, the griddle plate, extra pellets — is genuinely "it depends," not a must-buy.
