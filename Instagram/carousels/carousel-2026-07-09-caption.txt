Flour fails. Semolina doesn't.

We've all had the pizza that stuck to the peel and folded on itself going into the oven. It's not bad luck — it's the wrong dust. Semolina's coarse granules act like ball bearings under the dough, while flour bonds to it as it warms up and turns tacky. A thin layer, a two-minute check, and a fast hand are all it takes to stop it from happening again.
