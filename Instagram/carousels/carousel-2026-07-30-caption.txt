Do you need a second peel? Short answer: no. Useful answer: it depends on your oven.

One launch peel gets dough in and out just fine — we cooked a dozen pizzas that way before buying a second. The turning peel earns its keep once you're running hot: in a Solo Stove Pi or an Ooni Koda, things move fast enough that you may need to turn a pizza around the 30-second mark, and one peel can't launch and spin at the same time. Peel it in with the launch peel, then use the turning peel to rotate it halfway through — you catch a dark bottom before it's a problem. Cooler ovens, around 700°F, don't need the upgrade — one peel handles the whole job without feeling rushed. Turning peels run $30 to $60. Our advice: buy one good launch peel, use it for ten cooks, then decide if a turning peel fits how you work.
