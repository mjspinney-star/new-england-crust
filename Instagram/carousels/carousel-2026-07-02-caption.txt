This is the pizza we wait all year for.

Grilled corn, charred poblano, melted mozzarella, no red sauce — just olive oil and whatever char the grill gives you. We make this exact pie once a season, when the farm stand corn actually smells like something and the poblanos aren't phoning it in. The heat on the pepper is a gamble every time, so we always taste a strip before it goes on the pie. Build it fast, too — grilled corn loses its sweetness within the hour.
