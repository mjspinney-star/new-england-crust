Hot honey isn't a default drizzle. It's a decision.

We keep a bottle on hand year-round, but we don't reach for it on every pie — it's a strong opinion, not a garnish. On something rich and salty, like pepperoni or a funky aged cheese, it cuts right through the grease. On a Margherita or a plate of delicate vegetables, it just steamrolls everything else on the pizza. And it only goes on after the bake — honey scorches fast above 700°F, so timing is half the trick.
