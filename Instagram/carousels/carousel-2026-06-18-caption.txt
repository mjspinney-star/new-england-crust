Your oven display is not telling you what you think it is.

The Ninja Woodfire reads air temperature — not stone temperature. On a typical cook, those two numbers can be 100°F apart or more. That gap is the difference between a crust that finishes in 90 seconds with a proper char and one that sits for four minutes going pale before the bottom finally catches up. We fixed it with a $20 infrared thermometer, and we haven't looked back since. Full guide linked in bio.
