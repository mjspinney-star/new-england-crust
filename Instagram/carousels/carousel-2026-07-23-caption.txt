Canned or fresh — the clams are the whole ballgame on a white clam pizza.

We've made a lot of these, and when one goes wrong (rubbery, fishy, over-salted), the clams are almost always why. The short version: canned chopped clams are more forgiving in a 900°F+ oven because they're already cooked, and the reserved juice makes a great garlic oil base. Fresh littlenecks taste better, but they need to go on late — last 20 seconds — or they tighten up fast. Our rule is simple: weeknight cook, canned. Weekend with time and good company, fresh, shucked that morning.
