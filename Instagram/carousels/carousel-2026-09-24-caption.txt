We keep this page updated because oven prices don't move in a straight line — but the ovens worth watching don't change much, and neither do the price points that mean it's an actual deal.

Ooni Koda 12: 15 minutes to 800°F on the stone, 60-90 second cook. Below $280 is a strong buy signal, and it's dipped to $250 in past sales. Ninja Woodfire OO101: pizza in 4-7 minutes instead of 90 seconds, no true Neapolitan char, but it also smokes a pork shoulder — price swings $299 to $449, and the low end during a sale is worth moving on. Solo Stove Pi Prime is the most forgiving oven on the list, built for a relaxed cook over a fast one. Cuisinart's 3-in-1 costs $100-$150 less than the others and is a clear buy under $200.

One accessory worth grabbing whenever it's on sale: a $20 infrared thermometer. It reads your actual stone temperature, not the air temperature your oven's display shows — that gap is where bad pizza comes from.
