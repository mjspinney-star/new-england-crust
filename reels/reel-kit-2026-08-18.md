REEL 3 — "Why More Sauce Ruins Your Pizza"

Hook style: contrarian

SLIDES

[Hook — 2-3 sec] — HEADLINE: More sauce does not mean better pizza.
[2-4 sec] — HEADLINE: A 12-inch pizza needs 3-4 tablespoons. Max.
SUBLINE: Go heavier and the center won't set — especially on a fast outdoor cook.
[2-4 sec] — HEADLINE: This sauce doesn't keep like jarred sauce.
SUBLINE: Two to three days in the fridge, then it's done.
[2-4 sec] — HEADLINE: Make it right before you cook, not ahead of time.
SUBLINE: Let it sit 20 minutes first — the salt pulls out moisture and opens the flavor.
[Close — 3 sec] — Full breakdown → newenglandcrust.com / 🔥 New England Crust

CAPTION
The instinct is to load up on sauce. Don't. A few tablespoons is plenty, and this one's meant to be made fresh the day you cook — not stocked up in the fridge for later.
