CAPTION
We cooked bad pizza for a long time before we figured out the display on the Ninja Woodfire shows air temperature — not stone temperature. Those two numbers can be 100°F apart, and that gap is the reason for pale, dense bottoms. A $20 infrared thermometer is the fix. Point it at the center of the stone, wait until it reads your target, then launch.

REEL 3 — "Don't Launch Yet"
Source post: How to Read Your Pizza Stone with an Infrared Thermometer
Blog URL: newenglandcrust.com/blog/reading-your-stone-with-an-infrared-thermometer
Hook style: contrarian
Best posting time: Tuesday 7:00 PM

SLIDES
1. [Hook — 2-3 sec]
   HEADLINE: Your oven says it's ready. It's not.

2. [3 sec]
   HEADLINE: The "ready" signal means the air is hot.
   SUBLINE: The stone is still 80–120°F behind. Launch now and you'll get pale, stuck pizza.

3. [3 sec]
   HEADLINE: Read the center of the stone — not the edges.
   SUBLINE: The edges run hotter. Aim at center or you'll get an uneven cook.

4. [3 sec]
   HEADLINE: Launch when the center is within 25°F of your target.
   SUBLINE: Not when the display hits a number. When the stone hits a number.

5. [Close — 3 sec] — Full breakdown → newenglandcrust.com /
   🔥 New England Crust

CAPTION
The Ninja Woodfire tells you it's ready way before the stone actually is. We ignored the signal and started measuring the stone ourselves — and it changed everything. An extra 5–8 minutes of patience after the ready signal is the difference between consistent results and a coin flip every cook night.
