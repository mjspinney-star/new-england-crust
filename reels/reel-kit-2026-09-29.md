CAPTION
We used to just trust the oven's dial and hope for the best. Now we don't launch anything until an infrared thermometer says the stone itself is close to 540°F — the air reading and the stone reading aren't the same thing, and only one of them actually cooks your crust. It's a 10-second check that saves the bake.
