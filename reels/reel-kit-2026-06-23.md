REEL 1 — "We Wasted the Good Stuff"
Source post: Finishing with Good Olive Oil: When It Matters and When It Does Not

Hook style: mistake/reveal
Best posting time: Thursday 6:30 PM

SLIDES
1. [Hook — 2-3 sec]
   HEADLINE: We wasted expensive olive oil on pizza for months.

2. [3 sec]
   HEADLINE: Loaded toppings bury it completely.
   SUBLINE: Fennel sausage, two cheeses, roasted peppers — that finishing oil disappears. You won't taste a thing.

3. [3 sec]
   HEADLINE: Margherita is where it earns its place.
   SUBLINE: Simple ingredients, nothing to hide it. A real drizzle after the cut adds something you can actually taste.

4. [3 sec]
   HEADLINE: Drizzle right off the stone — never on the peel.
   SUBLINE: On the peel it smokes and burns. Off the stone, residual heat warms the oil without wrecking it.

5. [Close — 3 sec] — Full breakdown → newenglandcrust.com /
   🔥 New England Crust

CAPTION
We went through some genuinely expensive olive oil on pizzas that had no room for it before we figured this out. Finishing oil isn't a universal upgrade — it depends entirely on what you're putting it on. Simple pies with space for the oil to speak: yes. Loaded, heavily sauced pies: save it.

REEL 3 — "Stop Putting Oil on Everything"
Source post: Finishing with Good Olive Oil: When It Matters and When It Does Not
Blog URL: newenglandcrust.com/blog/finishing-with-good-olive-oil-when-it-matters-and-when-it-does-not
Hook style: contrarian
Best posting time: Tuesday 7:00 PM

SLIDES
1. [Hook — 2-3 sec]
   HEADLINE: Stop finishing every pizza with expensive olive oil.

2. [3 sec]
   HEADLINE: Heavily sauced pies kill the flavor.
   SUBLINE: Tomato sauce acidity and sugar bury whatever nuance you paid for in that bottle. It's invisible.

3. [3 sec]
   HEADLINE: Loaded pies are the same problem.
   SUBLINE: On a busy pizza night with four pies on the go, nobody is noticing the finishing oil anyway. That's the weeknight reality.

4. [3 sec]
   HEADLINE: Keep two bottles — use them separately.
   SUBLINE: One everyday oil for dough and sauce. One smaller, better bottle strictly for finishing. That separation changes how you use both.

5. [Close — 3 sec] — Full breakdown → newenglandcrust.com /
   🔥 New England Crust

CAPTION
The good finishing oil is worth it — on the right pizza. But we used it on everything for a while before we realized most of those drizzles were invisible. Heavily sauced, heavily topped pies have no room for it. Save it for the simple ones where bread and cheese are doing the work.
